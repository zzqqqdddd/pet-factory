# Raven Voyager look mechanics

Raven is a humanoid with a black high ponytail and straight bangs. Direction is conveyed by nose-tip and pupil placement, cheek/face plane, and the ponytail plus fringe silhouette. Keep the jacket, skirt, and boots stable in scale and baseline; never rotate the entire body to fake gaze.

Cardinal order is 000 up/back, 090 screen-right, 180 down/front, 270 screen-left. Intermediate views rotate in even clockwise 22.5-degree increments.
